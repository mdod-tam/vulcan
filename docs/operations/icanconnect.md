# iCanConnect costs and filing preparation

The normal application review covers identity, Maryland residency, income and disability certification. There is no separate iCanConnect application or approval. An approved DeafBlind applicant with approved income proof and disability certification can receive ICC-funded equipment. The application uses the constituent's hearing and vision disability information with those reviewed documents; catalog product groups describe products, not the applicant.

Funding belongs to the device or service. The same application can have MAT-funded and ICC-funded devices. Each bid and purchase order has one funding source, which carries through to its devices. ICC purchases go directly to beneficiaries; purchases for shelf stock stay MAT-funded. Using a MAT stock device for a DeafBlind constituent keeps that device MAT-funded.

Record MAT or iCanConnect funding on each assessment or training cost. Completing a report does not establish a reimbursement claim or supply a price. Finance can record the actual bill afterward. Training records include the date performed, billable hours and rate, mileage, invoice or timesheet, and devices used. A session covering several devices has one charge. ICC-funded training sessions appear in filing preparation and its worksheet alongside equipment and assessment costs. Missing costs or documents remain visible for staff to complete.

## Recording costs

MSRP is catalog information. At bid award, record the winning vendor, PO number, and price per device; each line price applies to every unit on that line. Vendor suggestions come from previously recorded POs. Unknown prices stay blank and can be completed with the invoice later. Device identifiers are recorded later in shipping and financial records. Actual device costs, purchase order shipping and other charges, invoice totals, and receipt and installation dates are recorded separately. A shipment does not supply a constituent receipt date. The invoice must reconcile to all device costs and shared charges before its costs are ready for filing. Device costs and shared PO charges are separate rows so staff can track exactly what was filed.

Finance records actual hourly rates and billable hours on each assessment or training session. Under **System Policies → Mileage rates**, enter the cost per mile and its **Effective from** date. Each rate applies from that date until the next rate takes effect, so January, July and other changes use the same form. Future rates can be entered ahead of time. The financial editor starts with the latest rate effective on or before the service date and saves it with the record; later settings changes do not change previously recorded costs. Mileage reimbursement is miles × the saved rate, rounded to cents, and is added to the hourly service charge. The worksheet lists miles, rate and mileage cost separately. Leave an unknown rate blank; a service with mileage cannot be filed until its rate is entered. Billable preparation or travel time is separate from session duration. Link the supplied devices used during training. Training may cover MAT-funded and ICC-funded devices. Record which devices were used; missing devices or receipt dates still need review. Installation, interpretation and travel bills use an application service expense linked to the beneficiary's ICC-funded device. Agency overhead stays in agency accounting.

To enter shared PO charges, open the purchase order and choose **Edit PO invoice and charges**. Enter shipping and any other invoice charges separately from the device prices. Each financial editor shows **Financial history** with the administrator, time, and recorded changes, including initial entries and later corrections. Related changes also appear in the application's activity history.

For orders shipping directly to constituents, confirm the vendor's shipping email and choose **Send PO to vendor**. The vendor can use the same secure link to view that order and record each package as it ships. The link expires after 30 days and staff can replace or revoke it from the PO. Staff can also record tracking from **Awaiting shipment**.

## Filing workflow

Open **iCanConnect filing preparation** from the Admin Dashboard and choose the date range and category. Filing preparation is a global workspace; each device on an application shows its own MAT or ICC funding label. **Unclaimed expenses** shows costs that have not been marked filed, including rows with missing information. **Period activity, including filed costs** includes previously filed costs. Equipment uses constituent receipt dates, services use performed dates, and shared PO charges use the last device receipt date. The worksheet keeps request, invoice, performed, receipt and installation dates separate. Unknown amounts stay blank.

The CSV worksheet and document ZIP help staff prepare the external filing. The ZIP contains the worksheet and each referenced current, cleared document once. The filing screen, worksheet, and ZIP use the same document reference. Earlier versions are available in the private financial record history.

After filing externally, select the exact costs included and record the filing period and external reference. The server locks and rechecks the selected records, amounts, documents and readiness before saving their filed status. The audit event records which document supported each cost, including when a later correction replaces that document. Filing status is recorded for each selected cost.

Filed costs and shared invoice evidence resist ordinary changes and deletion. An administrator correcting a filed cost must record a reason and external correction reference. Replaced filed invoices remain attached privately as superseded evidence. Do not change clinical or receipt dates to fit a claim. Financial attachments and packages use the existing cleared-upload and authenticated-download controls.

## Later income updates and MAT income settings

Normal application approval is the only initial person review. If someone has already received ICC equipment and seeks more ICC equipment or services a year or more after their last approved income review, request updated income proof from the application's existing Income Proof section. The date comes from ordinary `ProofReview` history; there is no duplicate ICC verification date or person-approval record. Previously eligible services use their actual service dates.

Send the normal secure income-upload link to the constituent or managing guardian through the existing recipient chooser. Delivery status, expiry, resend/revocation and scan checks use the normal secure-form workflow. Admins receive in-app notices when income proof is requested and when it arrives. Uploaded evidence appears in the regular proof-review queue; the existing delayed review reminder also includes it. The application remains approved while staff waits, reviews or rejects an income update. MAT-funded work can continue. Earlier income documents stay private alongside their review history.

If MAT uses vouchers and makes income optional, the MAT voucher can be issued while staff request or review income proof for iCanConnect. Once the proof is approved, staff can create an iCanConnect bid and purchase order on the same application. Each path keeps its own eligibility rules and records.
