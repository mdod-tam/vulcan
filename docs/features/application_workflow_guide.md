# Application Workflow

An `Application` holds one constituent's application to receive accessible telecommunications equipment and/or a voucher.

After submission, staff review the proofs and disability certification that came with it.

Approval then moves the application toward issuance of equipment or of a voucher.

Portal and paper intake share the same application model and lifecycle methods, but differ in their identity checks, form behavior, and follow-up messages.

## How applications enter the system

| Path | Owner and behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Portal draft/autosave | [AutosaveService](../../app/services/applications/autosave_service.rb) resumes or creates the actor's draft and saves individual non-file fields. |
| Portal save/submission | [ApplicationCreator](../../app/services/applications/application_creator.rb) validates the form, locks/rechecks participants and eligibility, saves applicant/application changes and attachments, then records history. Final submission moves the draft to `in_progress`. |
| Staff paper intake | [PaperApplicationService](../../app/services/applications/paper_application_service.rb) coordinates applicant selection/creation, proofs, the application write, and follow-up. Guardian quick-create is a separate step; see [paper intake](../development/paper_application_architecture.md). |

Portal final submission is blocked while the applicant is the subject of an open `registration_soft_match` review case. Draft saves and autosave can continue. The submission gate runs before applicant/application mutation; draft ownership, waiting-period rules, and conflicting applications are also checked under lock.

`ApplicationCreator` does not send its own notifications. Paper intake has explicit post-save communications and warns when they fail. Avoid assuming the two entry points have identical side effects.

## Draft autosave and reporting

[AutosaveService](../../app/services/applications/autosave_service.rb) resumes or creates a draft using the same actor/applicant rule as the portal form and full Save. It saves allowlisted fields and records the last successful field in `applications.last_visited_step`. Despite its name, this is an attribute such as `household_size`, not a form page. Application-field saves update the marker with `update_column`, which skips validations and callbacks. Disability-field saves persist it with `save!(validate: false)`, which skips validations but runs callbacks. Failed or unsupported field saves do not advance it; file uploads use a separate flow.

The admin **Pain Point Analysis** report at `/admin/application_analytics/pain_points` uses [Application.pain_point_analysis](../../app/models/application.rb) to count current drafts by that attribute, excluding blank markers. These counts are a clue for investigation: there is no inactivity cutoff, and a draft doesn't show that someone abandoned the application, or why they stopped.

The [autosave controller tests](../../test/controllers/constituent_portal/applications_controller_autosave_test.rb) and [report tests](../../test/controllers/admin/application_analytics_controller_test.rb) cover the server side; browser behavior is in [JavaScript architecture](../development/javascript_architecture.md#autosave-as-a-representative-interaction).

## Status and approval

[Application#transition_status!](../../app/models/application.rb) owns lifecycle changes: the status update, the status-history row, and the `application_status_changed` audit call happen together in one transaction. A transition to the current status is a no-op and writes nothing.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `draft` | The constituent is still preparing the application. |
| `in_progress` | Submitted and being processed. |
| `awaiting_proof`, `reminder_sent` | Waiting for documents or recording a reminder. |
| `awaiting_dcf` | Waiting for the disability certification form. |
| `approved`, `rejected`, `archived` | Approved, declined, or retained as historical work. |

These are available states, not a promise that every transition between them is allowed by every workflow.

Admin batch approval/rejection is all-or-nothing. A database or required-audit failure aborts the batch before the controller translates the error; it does not continue running records in an aborted PostgreSQL transaction. A failure after commit is reported as committed with follow-up guidance when the batch's history receipts confirm it. If that readback fails, staff are told to inspect history before retrying.

Approval/rejection records staff workflow without sending constituent notices or approval letters. Existing voucher-assignment notices and requests for replacement proof or required information have their own delivery rules. Evaluation requests recheck eligibility and pending state under the application lock and commit the request timestamp with its audit event.

Manual and automatic approval require the application's saved proof requirements and approved disability certification. Rejected and archived applications cannot return to active processing through an ordinary status transition. The admin profile editor does not change application status; lifecycle changes use the canonical transition owner.

[Application#reconcile_workflow_state!](../../app/models/concerns/application_status_management.rb) reevaluates progress after document work:

- Approved residency and ID proofs, plus income proof when required, satisfy the regular proof requirements.
- When those proofs are approved and certification is outstanding, reconciliation can move the application to `awaiting_dcf`. It does not request certification: staff send the request from the application page, which shows certification as Not Requested until then.
- When required proofs and disability certification are approved, reconciliation can approve the application.
- Already approved, rejected, or archived applications are left alone.

Fulfillment type and whether income proof is required are stamped at creation from feature settings. Use the application's stored requirements for its subsequent review.

## Admin application lists

The Applications page opens **Review applications** for submitted, unapproved work and approved applications with income-proof updates awaiting review. **All applications** also includes drafts, approved, rejected, and archived records. Click the Applicant, Created, Status, or Next action column heading to sort; click again to reverse it. Status sorts by time in the current status, with unknown dates last. The clock uses the latest lifecycle entry into that status, so ordinary edits do not reset it and re-entry starts a new stay. Changing Status clears a task filter; search, date, and sort changes keep that filter.

The main Dashboard shows in-progress task counts for applications awaiting proof uploads or reminders, proofs ready for staff review, certifications needing a request, certifications requested from providers, and certifications ready for review. Requests are still needed for applications awaiting certification with no request or signed form recorded. The certification-review count combines received documents and digitally signed certifications, counting each application once. Task counts can overlap and do not partition the application total. Its Approved card also combines an independent training count with equipment stages; completed shipments are available through shipment history. Each task row links to its matching application list. The evaluation and bid links combine their two respective pipeline stages, and awaiting training includes both unassigned requests and open sessions.

Review cards open the proof and certification queues. Certification review includes both received documents and digitally signed certifications, with separate filters for each. These cards count applications, including an application only once in each card. Longest waits use current document receipt dates; replacements start a new wait for the replaced document. Approval averages use recorded decisions matched to that same document's receipt, with the sample count shown. Missing dates remain unrecorded.

The Dashboard's Approved link opens **Approved pipeline** on the same page. [Equipment::FulfillmentStage](../../app/models/equipment/fulfillment_stage.rb) supplies seven action cards: **Assign evaluator**, **Enter evaluation report**, **Needs bid number**, **Enter PO details**, **Record PO sent date**, **Enter shipping info**, and **View shipment history**. Each application counts once at its earliest unfinished equipment obligation. Secondary quantities and their oldest waits remain visible, so a new request does not hide an overdue shipment. Application status remains Approved. Shipment history is completed work, rather than another task to perform.

The separate **Assign trainer** card opens the training dashboard and counts approved pending requests without an assigned trainer. It shows the oldest request with the constituent's disability group; that name opens the request's assignment row on the training dashboard. Its average measures recorded request-to-assignment time, not the age of pending requests. It does not use the equipment pipeline's filters or create another equipment stage. Already assigned and scheduled work remains on the training dashboard. The main Dashboard's broader awaiting-training count includes those open sessions.

For equipment and vouchers, each card's title and count filter the application list. The constituent name beneath the longest wait opens that application's next action: evaluator assignment in **Evaluation and Training Status**, its assigned evaluation report, its equipment requests, the PO needing a sent date (or its PO list when several are involved), or its **Enter shipping info** queue. Report-wait cards also name the evaluator. These name links use the same destination as the row's **Next action** link. Shipment links filter by stock or vendor only when all pending devices share that source; mixed-source work shows both. Status cards appear above the filters, with filters immediately before the application rows.

**Enter PO details** opens the PO-entry form directly when one bid is awaiting award. When several bids are involved, it opens that application's bid list so staff can choose the bid. The form retains a **Back to Bid** link for reviewing reservations.

Voucher applications have their own group, including Voucher issued and Expired with unused value. Cancelled and fully used vouchers do not enter the expired-value follow-up queue. No equipment needed is a separate group for an explicit equipment decision; training links appear when training was requested or recorded.

**Approved during** filters by approval time, independently of application creation and stage age. It defaults to All time, with 30/60/90/120-day and 12-month options. Cards and the matching list use the same filtered cohort. Pending cards show longest and average waits; completed work shows completion dates and sorts newest first. Assignment, need, bid-line, PO-link, sent, allocation and shipment facts own the clocks. Unknown dates are identified and excluded from averages.

**Awaiting provider signature** is separate from certification review: it lists requested certifications that have not returned. Its wait starts at the latest confirmed DocuSeal handoff or accepted provider-request email. Queuing mail or creating a secure link does not establish a send date; those requests remain counted with the date marked unrecorded.

## Evaluation and training dashboards

The training dashboard separates unassigned requests (**Assign trainer**, for administrators) from assigned work awaiting an appointment (**Schedule training**). Administrators select an active trainer and press **Assign trainer** in the request row. The evaluation dashboard also provides inline **Assign evaluator** controls for equipment applications needing an evaluation and explicit pending evaluation requests. Assignment uses the existing services and eligibility rules, records its audit history and notifications, and returns to the dashboard with a result message. Trainers and evaluators see only their own work and cannot assign other staff. Requested and scheduled work remain separate from unassigned requests.

Administrators have four action cards: assignment, scheduling, report entry and **Review follow-up**. Follow-up opens cancelled visits and no-shows that need review for rescheduling. Completed work remains in the recent completed section and its **View all** list.

The cards show the oldest constituent and disability group with a link to the relevant record, plus the assigned trainer or evaluator. Training tables and completed sessions name the trainer recorded on that session. Scheduling waits begin at the recorded assignment, or at a new request when a trainer already covers the application. Scheduled waits begin when the appointment was booked; a future appointment date is not an elapsed-wait clock. Averages measure recorded request-to-assignment, assignment-to-scheduling, or scheduling-to-actual-visit intervals, as labeled, with sample counts. Missing history stays unrecorded.

## Documents have two review paths

**Income, residency, and ID:** [ProofAttachmentService](../../app/services/proof_attachment_service.rb) and [ProofReview](../../app/models/proof_review.rb) own attachment/review behavior. Approval requires a file; rejection can record a missing document. Rejection delivery goes through `Applications::RequestProofResubmission`, which issues a secure upload request. A delivery failure does not erase the saved review.

**Disability certification:** provider requests, secure uploads, staff uploads, and DocuSeal have their own services. Certification progresses through `not_requested`, `requested`, `received`, `approved`, or `rejected`. Every change goes through `Application#transition_disability_certification_status!`, which writes the status, its `ApplicationStatusChange` row, and its audit event in one transaction; a repeated status, such as a second rejection, is still recorded. Receipt or a completed signature is separate from approval. Incoming fax/postal documents require staff upload. Staff upload is unavailable while a certification awaits review (`received`) or is approved, so it cannot replace one; it returns once a certification is rejected. `DisabilityCertificationAttachmentService.attach_certification` checks this under the same application lock a provider submission takes, so a submission that arrives mid-request is not displaced. Secure provider forms follow their own request rules.

See [proof review](proof_review_process_guide.md) and [DocuSeal integration](../development/docuseal_integration_guide.md) for those workflows.

## Guardians, communication, and history

For a dependent application, `application.user` is the dependent and `managing_guardian` identifies the responsible guardian. A submitted ID alone does not authorize a relationship. Contact ownership determines where a message can go; see [guardian relationships](../development/guardian_relationship_system.md).

Communications may create notification history, email, printable letters, or workflow-specific SMS. Some actions, including individual proof approval, are intentionally record-only. Start with [notifications](notifications.md) rather than adding a mailer call at each lifecycle step.

The application timeline combines several record types. It filters and deduplicates them for display; it is not the complete raw audit dataset. See [audit events](audit_event_tracking.md).

Staff deactivate a user from the admin user page to stop account access while preserving application and audit history. Deactivation revokes existing sessions and records the responsible administrator. Reactivation is an explicit, audited action; it does not restore revoked sessions. User deletion is not an admin operation, and model restrictions prevent deletion of users or applications that retain history.

## Program policies

[`Policy`](../../app/models/policy.rb) stores income thresholds, waiting periods, training limits, request throttles, secure-link timing, and voucher amounts. `bin/rails db:bootstrap` initializes these ([bootstrapping an environment](../infrastructure/setup_and_maintenance.md#bootstrapping-an-environment)), and `bin/rails db:seed_policies` reapplies the checked-in defaults, replacing any values that differ.

Inspect the configured values from the command line:

```bash
bin/rails runner 'pp Policy.order(:key).pluck(:key, :value).to_h'
```

Routine changes go through `/admin/policies`. For a console update, set `policy.updated_by` to the responsible administrator before `policy.update!(value: ...)` so the `PolicyChange` records the operator. Initialization defaults live in [Baseline::Policies](../../app/services/baseline/policies.rb), which the bootstrap, the task, and the demo seed all use.

## Fulfillment settings

At creation, [Application#stamp_workflow_defaults!](../../app/models/application.rb) saves the fulfillment type and income-proof requirement. With `vouchers_enabled` on, it selects voucher fulfillment and no income-proof requirement; with it off, it selects equipment fulfillment and requires income proof.

[FeatureFlag.income_proof_required?](../../app/models/feature_flag.rb) is the inverse of `vouchers_enabled`, not a separate feature-flag row. Approval uses the application's saved requirement, so changing the flag does not rewrite existing applications.

`bin/rails db:bootstrap` and `bin/rails db:seed_feature_flags` set `vouchers_enabled` to disabled, along with the other flag defaults. Inspect it with `bin/rails features:list`; use `bin/rails 'features:enable[vouchers_enabled]'` or `bin/rails 'features:disable[vouchers_enabled]'` when deliberately changing fulfillment mode.

## Voucher fulfillment

Approval of a voucher application queues [IssueInitialVoucherJob](../../app/jobs/issue_initial_voucher_job.rb) after commit. The two approval paths queue it explicitly: [Applications::Approver](../../app/services/applications/approver.rb) for staff approval and `Application#reconcile_workflow_state!` for automatic approval. `transition_status!` writes status, history and audit only. [Vouchers::IssuanceService](../../app/services/vouchers/issuance_service.rb) checks the feature flag, eligibility, and existing voucher before issuance.

Equipment applications do not create vouchers. Vendor redemption is a separate workflow through `Vouchers::RedemptionService`; see [voucher controls](../security/voucher_security_controls.md).

## Evaluator workflow

Administrator interfaces remain English for v1. The evaluator's appointment and report forms retain their existing English copy; shared contact and product guidance labels follow the viewer's supported language.

After approval, staff assign an active evaluator from the application's **Evaluation and Training Status** section, within the service window. Equipment fulfillment starts with an evaluation; voucher applications need one only when staff mark evaluator support as needed. Administrators can also be assigned as evaluators. [Applications::EvaluatorAssignment](../../app/services/applications/evaluator_assignment.rb) owns assignment and reassignment; [EvaluationManagement](../../app/models/concerns/evaluation_management.rb) keeps the evaluation lookups.

The assignment sends a notice to the evaluator and appears in their requested queue at `/evaluators/dashboard`. The evaluator opens the record, contacts the applicant or the application's managing guardian, agrees on an appointment, and enters its future date/time and any location or scheduling notes. Scheduling and rescheduling record arrangements; the evaluator shares those arrangements directly with the applicant. Missing email or phone is shown as unavailable and does not prevent the evaluator assignment notice.

Assigned evaluators can open **View Applicant Information** for the applicant's contact details, address, disabilities, communication preferences, managing guardian, and alternate contact. Dependent contact choices resolve each field within that application's guardian context, with the contact owner identified. This summary excludes financial information, proof documents, and administrative controls. Administrators retain the full application view, including administrators acting as evaluators. Appointment and report mutations remain restricted to the assigned evaluator; standalone evaluation creation and information-request writes are not available through the evaluator portal.

Before report submission, the assigned evaluator can save **Working Notes** for outreach attempts and assessment preparation. This saves only that internal notes section; it leaves the appointment, lifecycle status, and report unchanged. Notes remain available to program staff and the replacement evaluator after reassignment. After submission, saved notes appear as **Pre-report working notes** and are read-only; an empty pre-report notes section is omitted. Later observations use **Supplemental internal notes**, separate from the locked **Report internal notes**. All three internal notes fields are excluded from constituent confirmation messages.

After the appointment, the evaluator records the actual appointment date/time, location, **Report internal notes**, and recommendations. Select recommended catalog products using checkboxes and describe any non-catalog products or other recommendation details in the text field; both can appear in one report. If nothing suitable was found, select **No suitable product was found** and explain why. This cannot be combined with catalog recommendations. Each product marked as tried requires a **Satisfied**, **Neutral**, or **Dissatisfied** rating. Direct assessments recommending catalog products require at least one trial; outside-catalog and no-suitable-product reports may have none. Other attendees are optional. Recommendation details are shared verbatim in the confirmation email or letter to the applicant or managing guardian; report notes stay internal. Program staff review outside-catalog recommendations and no-suitable-product decisions before deciding equipment fulfillment.

An assigned administrator can choose **Outside-specialist consultation** on a requested, scheduled, or confirmed evaluation. Record the actual consultation completion date, written source/context in report notes, and the recommendation outcome. This path does not require a future appointment, a planned date, a location, or claimed product trials; an existing planned appointment remains recorded, and known trials can still be recorded. The choice is restricted to the assigned administrator. An administrator viewing somebody else's report cannot submit it, and an ordinary evaluator cannot use the consultation exception.

The actual appointment time must not be in the future, even when the visit happened earlier than planned. Validation errors preserve entered fields, recommendation details, product selections and satisfaction ratings for correction and retry. A focused error summary links to the affected controls. Submission completes and locks the report, records its audit event, and requests a confirmation using the resolved delivery contact. The evaluator can subsequently add or update supplemental notes without changing the submitted report.

The three times have different meanings: `evaluation_date` is the planned appointment, `appointment_completed_at` is the evaluator-entered actual appointment, and `report_submitted_at` is captured by the server during successful report submission. Completed lists sort by report submission time. Historical report submission times are restored from their matching completion audit events when available; unknown actual or submission times retain that uncertainty rather than being inferred from the schedule or later edits.

Application messages use the shared [application contact context](../../app/services/applications/contact_context.rb). For a dependent, the application's managing guardian determines email versus letter preference; the selected email or mailing-address owner determines language. Secure-request links retain their explicit channel and persisted recipient snapshots. If the selected email route has no usable contact, delivery is recorded as not sent with a missing-recipient reason; it does not prevent report completion or send to another guardian or alternate contact.

The evaluator can cancel with a reason, mark a no-show after the scheduled time, or reschedule. Cancelled and no-show records appear in **Needs Follow-up**, where the assigned evaluator can arrange a future appointment. Within the service window, staff can open **Reassign Evaluator**, select the replacement and confirm the transfer in a dialog. Cancelling the dialog leaves the assignment unchanged. Practitioner choices show the staff member's account email alongside their name to distinguish people with the same name, with a record ID when no email is available. The appointment, outcome, notes, products, and history remain on the same record, both evaluator identities are audited, and the replacement is notified. The replacement receives portal access and confirms the arrangements directly with the applicant; the former evaluator loses access unless they retain administrator oversight permissions. Only an unfinished evaluation transfers: a completed or cancelled one cannot, and a completed evaluation elsewhere on the application does not block the transfer of a separate open one.

[Evaluation services](../../app/services/evaluations) lock the application before the evaluation and recheck approval, assignment, and lifecycle state before scheduling, rescheduling, or completing a report. A stale submission or appointment action cannot overwrite a completed report or act on a transferred assignment. Completed reports remain available after the application is archived. [Evaluator request tests](../../test/controllers/evaluator/evaluations_controller_test.rb), [access tests](../../test/controllers/evaluator/evaluation_access_test.rb), and [reassignment tests](../../test/models/evaluation_reassignment_test.rb) cover these boundaries.

## Trainer workflow and shared reports

The trainer dashboard panels, session lists, appointment/report forms, shared contact labels, and training notices support English and Spanish. Existing portal navigation and layout titles retain their current copy. Constituent training history and product guidance follow the viewer's supported language. Stored report text retains its authored language; the portal does not translate it automatically.

Dashboard counts include every matching item, even when a session preview shows only the first ten. Unassigned requests appear under **Assign trainer**; assigned requests awaiting an appointment appear in the scheduling table. The scheduled-session list includes both upcoming appointments and past appointments awaiting an outcome; the overdue preview reports its total and links to that list.

After approval, staff assign an active eligible trainer in the application's **Training** block, within the service window and session quota. [Applications::TrainerAssignment](../../app/services/applications/trainer_assignment.rb) stores current coverage in `Application#assigned_trainer` and creates the requested appointment together; [TrainingManagement](../../app/models/concerns/training_management.rb) keeps the quota and eligibility reads. A trainer cannot silently replace existing coverage. Administrators can remove coverage at quota or between appointments; removal cancels any open appointment, retains private notes and completed reports, and clears the assignment. Administrators can instead transfer the application's open training (requested, scheduled, confirmed and rescheduled rows, and a no-show still awaiting its follow-up) to another eligible trainer with **Reassign Trainer**: schedules, locations, notes and outcomes stay on the same rows, completed sessions and a no-show that a later session resolved stay with their trainers, and coverage moves with them atomically. When the covering trainer can no longer take training and nothing is open, the same action moves the coverage alone and sends no appointment notices. Pressing Escape cannot dismiss the transfer dialog while its request is pending. The form carries the coverage the admin reviewed, so a changed assignment is refused instead of overwritten. The service window still applies, and the page says so when it blocks a transfer. `Applications::TrainerAssignment.reassign` owns the transfer; the replacement and the constituent are notified after commit.

The current trainer opens the requested session, reviews the application's coordinating contact, latest evaluation recommendations, previous reports, and known supplied products, then agrees on an appointment with the constituent or managing guardian. The contact card uses [Applications::ContactContext](../../app/services/applications/contact_context.rb) rather than assuming the applicant owns every contact field. Requested appointments can be scheduled or cancelled. New appointment dates start blank, and entry/display identify the application time zone. Known supplied products are suggestions; the trainer selects the actual model used, including a legitimate demonstration model.

There is one unresolved requested, scheduled, or confirmed appointment per application. For a scheduled appointment, **What happened?** offers a completed report, cancellation, a no-show after its planned start, or rescheduling for a constituent-requested change, including a change recorded after the original start. Rescheduling requires a reason and a different future agreed time. A late-entered cancellation does not become a no-show automatically. Outcomes cannot overwrite an existing completed, cancelled, or no-show outcome. The trainer records an outcome before creating another appointment: follow-up starts from the latest cancelled/no-show appointment, and an additional appointment starts from the latest completed report. Current coverage, application eligibility, the service window, remaining quota, and no open appointment are rechecked under lock. Capacity and an elapsed service window do not prevent closing an existing appointment truthfully.

A completed report requires the trained product, positive duration, actual `completed_at`, a deliberately authored `constituent_summary`, and its `summary_locale`. Actual completion must be between creation of the training request and the present; an early visit can be reported before its planned appointment time. The server records `report_submitted_at` separately. Optional `internal_notes` hold private preparation and observations; they are never copied into a shared summary. Omitted notes retain the existing preparation text, while an explicitly submitted blank clears it. Rejected forms preserve attempted fields for correction, including explicit blanks, product/language selection, notes, summary, and times.

Normal completion and ordinary appointment cancellation retain trainer coverage. After coverage removal or transfer, the author can still read their completed report, but cannot use it to schedule new work or resolve current contact/preparation details. Administrators retain oversight through their authorized views. A constituent request during existing coverage goes to the current trainer through [TrainingRequestService](../../app/services/applications/training_request_service.rb); uncovered requests, and requests whose covering trainer can no longer take training, go to administrators for assignment. Removing coverage or cancelling an appointment also supersedes any transfer notice still queued for it.

The applicant or current managing guardian can open completed training history at `/constituent_portal/training_sessions` using the same application-access boundary as the portal. Shared reports show only the authored constituent summary and its stored language; private notes and outcome reasons stay on authorized staff surfaces. The summary is not machine-translated. Resources for the exact product trained on appear there even when training used a demonstration device absent from **Devices**.

Training commands save the outcome and its audit together, then arrange one notice through [TrainingSessionNotifier](../../app/services/training_session_notifier.rb) after enclosing transactions commit. Assignment notifies the trainer; appointment and completion communications use existing constituent/guardian email-or-letter routing. A queued or deferred notice does not confirm receipt. Suppression or delivery failure is reported separately from the saved appointment/report; do not repeat a committed save to retry delivery. Completion notices link to the authenticated shared report and materials only when the selected email or mailing-address owner can access that application. Other channel owners receive guidance to ask the managing guardian to sign in or contact program staff. Notices do not include private observations or outcome reasons.

## Devices and product guidance

The dashboard's **My equipment & help** card, **Devices** index, application equipment summary and product details use [Applications::ProductHistory](../../app/queries/applications/product_history.rb) in `portal_visible` mode over `Application.accessible_by(current_user)`. They include completed voucher redemption lines, shipped active units, sent direct orders, and received stock allocated to the beneficiary. Unsent POs, bid reservations, unallocated stock, recommendations, withdrawn units and training references grant no device access. Guardian-only accounts see managed beneficiaries without needing their own active application.

One exact product has one guidance entry with separate source identities, quantities and localized statuses: **Purchased with voucher**, **Order in progress**, **Preparing shipment from stock**, or **Shipped by the program**. In-progress sources may have no date; only actual shipment dates are labeled as shipments. A release/correction can remove pending guidance access. Outstanding demand then shows **Equipment fulfillment in progress**. Cancelled demand does not promise fulfillment. The `fulfilled` mode remains the historical/trainer rule: completed voucher purchases and actual shipments. Shipment does not confirm constituent receipt.

Staff manage public resources from **Resources** in product management, including archived products. A [ProductResource](../../app/models/product_resource.rb) has a descriptive title, explicit content language, and exactly one absolute HTTP(S) link or PDF, attached to that exact product. Resources are ordered by title within each language. Selecting a different source or PDF replacement takes effect only after a valid save; failed changes keep the prior resource/file and retain accepted uploads for correction. PDFs must identify as PDF content and be at most 10 MB. Public program guidance must not include personal, application, or session information. Authors should supply searchable/tagged PDFs, and captions/transcripts for externally hosted videos.

Devices, completed reports, and current trainer preparation share the same resource presentation. It prefers the viewer's exact language, labels other-language alternatives, and explains when preferred-language guidance is unavailable. An empty resource list has its own message. Hosted videos remain ordinary external links, with no embedded player. Existing manufacturer documentation is separately labeled catalog metadata; it does not substitute for localized guidance. Public guidance PDFs use signed Active Storage proxy downloads without resource-specific login checks; personalized device history and reports retain their authorization checks. See [file storage](../infrastructure/active_storage_s3_setup.md#public-product-guidance-pdfs).

## Equipment purchasing and shipment

Equipment purchasing preserves three separate facts: the submitted assessment recommendation, staff's current intended models and quantities, and each actual physical device. Equipment operations record fulfillment without changing application approval status.

Successful report completion initializes [EquipmentNeed](../../app/models/equipment_need.rb) once, within the report transaction, with one unit per selected catalog model. The application retains its source evaluation. Outside-catalog and no-suitable-product outcomes initialize no invented units and stay in **Needs bid number → Choose equipment for bid** until staff save an explicit decision. `equipment_needs_decided_at` records that decision, including a reasoned empty save. Reads do not recreate removed needs or alter the report.

**Edit equipment needs** owns quantities, each need's single disability setup group, the explicitly confirmed application program groups, and shipping defaults. Address suggestions use the application's contact context, including guardian households, but staff confirm the actual destination. Care-of and apartment/unit are separate. Direct award and stock allocation snapshot these values onto each physical unit; later profile edits do not rewrite them. Shipping fields are encrypted and protected in request logs and audit metadata while authorized operational pages show the complete destination.

At `/admin/equipment`, **Needs bid number** combines unreserved need quantities and detached direct units. Search/model/manufacturer/category/program-group filters operate within one unpaginated selection form. Checked hidden rows remain selected, and the summary names how many are hidden. **Enter bid number** reserves the explicit selected quantities or detached units under one mixed-model bid. Bids have one purpose, **Direct to constituents** or **Program stock**, and one funding source. Staff can confirm a reserved need's disability setup or adjust its quantity while keeping the same model and at least the reserved quantity. That change requires a fresh award review. Releasing all lines leaves the bid number open for reuse; **Cancel entire bid** closes it explicitly. Cancelled groups remain history. Reserved quantities count inside remaining need quantity and cannot be consumed again.

**Save PO details** awards every current bid line to one globally unique normalized PO number. Staff may explicitly reuse an unsent PO with matching purpose and funding. Award creates one physical [EquipmentItem](../../app/models/equipment_item.rb) per new unit, consumes only those need quantities, and reuses detached direct-unit IDs. A stale, unavailable or ineligible member rejects the transaction. The PO starts unsent. **Record PO sent date** records the actual nonfuture sent date separately and rejects empty orders. Direct shipment requires that boundary; tracking alone does not ship equipment.

Equipment navigation groups the work under **Needs bid number**, **Enter PO details**, and **Stock**. The oldest dated request, shipment, bid and unsent PO name a constituent with their disability group and a short device summary. Bid names open PO entry directly; unsent PO names open the order. A bid or order spanning several constituents shows one representative and counts the others; inventory purchases say **Program stock**. The Approved pipeline remains the application progress overview; its next-action links open the relevant work page.

The Equipment cards show totals across all applications and stock, independent of page filters. **Needs bid number** counts device-request rows still needing stock or a bid, and devices awaiting shipment. **Enter PO details** counts open bids with reservations and purchase orders missing a recorded sent date. Empty open bids remain accessible but do not count as awaiting award. Stock counts available, reserved and incoming devices. Fully bid-reserved requests are left out of the first count; a partially reserved request remains one row. Counts use their named units rather than treating bids, orders, requests and devices as interchangeable. Waits use the recorded queue dates and count completed 24-hour days, then whole weeks. Crossing midnight alone does not add a day. Missing dates remain unknown. An open bid's age starts when it was created; amendments do not restart that clock.

**Stock** supports both buying through the same bid/PO flow and recording verified existing stock with provenance. Retrying a submitted intake keeps the original inventory count. Open a new form for another intake. **Create a bid to buy stock** leads to quantities and a **Disability group** for each model, then **Review stock bid**. **Add another setup** adds a separate batch of the same model, such as 10 Deaf and 10 DeafBlind devices on one bid. Staff use these groups internally when receiving and preparing devices. **Unassigned** means there is no recorded setup group.

A stock purchase order buys devices for inventory and has an explicitly entered program receiving destination. **Receive into stock** opens the receipt form from the PO or the Stock queue. Each order line shows its model, setup, and bid. Enter the quantity that arrived for that line, or choose **Receive remaining** to fill the outstanding balance. Optional serial and IMEI fields appear for the entered quantity. A partial receipt leaves its remaining balance visible. If another receipt changes the counts, the form keeps your entries for the current lines and asks you to check them before saving.

Available stock means received, active, unallocated and unshipped units. Reserved for dispatch means received units allocated to an application; incoming means sent stock PO units not yet received. **Use stock** allocates the oldest received same-model units, then ID, without silently reducing reviewed quantities. A detached request is retained as a withdrawn demand row linked to its replacement stock unit. Release can restore the original need or detached demand once; cancellation does not restore unwanted demand. An explicit same-model swap moves the allocation while preserving each physical unit's source, acquisition PO, receipt and identifiers. Ordinary corrections cannot bypass reservations or transfer shipped devices.

**Enter shipping info** combines sent direct orders and received allocated stock. Packing shows the original ordered stock setup beside the required constituent setup, accepts missing identifiers, and requires setup confirmation for stock. Select the devices in that queue, then open **Enter shipping date and tracking** to enter the actual shipment date, carrier and tracking number. One shipment save covers one beneficiary, destination and source. **View shipment history** lists recorded shipments; it does not represent a remaining task. Existing stock can ship without an invented PO. Stock receipt and allocation never establish constituent shipment or receipt.

The **Supplier order list** includes exact models, quantities, and confirmed ship-to names and addresses. Open **Copy plain text list of all products in this PO** to copy those details with the PO number. Use the program receiving address for stock orders. If the supplier agreed to setup work, add the specific configuration instructions to your message. Disability groups and device identifiers belong in the internal receipt and packing workflows. **Device tracking and records** shows per-device application links, funding, identifiers, dates and correction history. **Edit price and receipt dates** opens the device's financial record. **Correct device or shipping details** opens an audited correction. **Edit PO invoice and charges** opens the shared invoice, document and shipping charges. Record the price paid separately from catalog MSRP and leave unknown prices blank.

Corrections retain device, PO and audit history and require reasons, current versions and reviewed impact where applicable. Detaching an unshipped direct unit retains that same unit without restoring need quantity. Stock release uses its separate restoration path. Active IMEI and manufacturer/model/serial uniqueness span available, allocated, direct and shipped equipment. Archived applications retain history but cannot receive new procurement assignments. Official carrier links remain navigation only.

Product MSRP is optional reference information. PO detail records actual cost per physical unit for either funding program. Unknown MSRP or actual costs stay blank; these tracking fields do not change procurement eligibility.

**Equipment by Type** counts active actual outbound shipments in the selected period across direct, purchased-stock and existing-stock sources, plus qualifying voucher purchases when requested. Acquisition PO dates, stock receipts, allocations and remaining needs do not count as constituent distribution.
## Two things that bite

A status written directly leaves no `ApplicationStatusChange` row and fires none of the transition's follow-up work, so the application ends up in a state its own history cannot explain. Timeline deduplication does not compensate: it hides duplicate events from the display while both rows remain stored.

Portal and paper intake share the lifecycle but not the surrounding behavior, so a rule changed in one place is only half changed.
